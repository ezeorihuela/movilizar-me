Privacy Notice

How we care for your data

MovilizarMe is the ministry of Ezequiel Gonzalez and his family, in Kalamazoo, Michigan, United States. This notice explains what data we collect on movilizar.me, what we use it for, and what rights you have over it.

What data we collect

  • What you give us yourself when you register for a workshop, seminar, or training: name, email address, and phone number.
  • Your booking details when you schedule a meeting: name, email, and the meeting details.
  • What you write when you contact us.

This site does not take payments or donations directly. Donations are made on the Mesa Global platform, so we never see or store your card details.

What we use it for

  • To reply to you and coordinate meetings, events, and training.
  • To send you ministry information only when you asked for it.
  • To keep a basic participant record for each activity.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising.

Who else processes data on this site

  • Google: the registration forms, the meeting schedule, email, and visit measurement (Google Analytics). Its notice is on Google's pages.
  • Mesa Global: donations and support for Gonzalez on Mission, on its official platform.
  • GitHub Pages: the hosting of this site. The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies. Google Analytics and the Google services embedded on some pages may use theirs to count visits, under their own policies.

Your rights

You may ask us at any time to access, correct, or delete your data. Write to us at ezequiel@movilizar.me and we will take care of it at no cost.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, we will publish the new version on this page with its date. Current version: November 25, 2026.
