Get Involved

Not all of us can go. All of us can take part.

This page follows the ideas of the XPLORE study, from the Center for Mission Mobilization: five habits of the world Christian, each with a practical step at MovilizarMe.

  1. 1. Pray

    Pray for the nations and for the peoples who do not yet know Jesus.

    Coordinate through a meeting

    Do you have a prayer request? Share it with us: we pray over every request we receive.

  2. 2. Send

    Sending means sustaining those who go: with giving, with care, and with steady support.

    Support through Mesa Global

    Your donation is processed securely on the official Mesa Global platform. MovilizarMe does not receive or store your card details.

  3. 3. Welcome

    The nations are already here, among neighbors, coworkers, and nearby families: welcoming is also mission.

    I want to welcome: first step

  4. 4. Go

    Go where the church is not yet: church planting and a new field sought in prayer.

    See church planting

  5. 5. Mobilize

    To mobilize is to awaken others: XPLORE in a small group, training with CESI, and the podcast for your church.

    Share the podcast

Take the first step

We want to open small groups with the XPLORE study. If you would like to take the study, or bring it to your church, write to us.
