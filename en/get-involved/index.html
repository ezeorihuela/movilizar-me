Get Involved

Not all of us can go. All of us can take part.

This page follows the ideas of the XPLORE study, from the Center for Mission Mobilization (CMM Press, today also Via Nations). XPLORE is a 7 lesson small group study that walks through God's Word, God's world, and God's work. Here we present only its general ideas, with attribution. The full lessons are CMM material and are not reproduced on this site.

The 7 lessons of XPLORE are: God's heart; God's world; praying; sending; welcoming; going; and mobilizing. From them come five habits of the world Christian. Each habit has a practical step at MovilizarMe:

  1. 1. Pray

    Pray for the nations and for the peoples who do not yet know Jesus. Join us in prayer, and in prayer walks through the city.

    Coordinate through a meeting

  2. 2. Send

    Sending means sustaining those who go: with giving, with care, and with steady support. Our family serves as workers with Mesa Global.

    Support through Mesa Global

  3. 3. Welcome

    The nations are already here: neighbors, coworkers, and families at our children's school, in Kalamazoo and across Michigan. Welcoming is also mission.

    I want to welcome: first step

  4. 4. Go

    Go where the church is not yet. Church planting, mission trips, and the new field we are seeking in prayer.

    See church planting

  5. 5. Mobilize

    To mobilize is to awaken others. Take the XPLORE study in a 7 week small group, train with CESI, and share the podcast with your church.

    Share the podcast
