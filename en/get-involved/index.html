Get Involved

Not all of us can go. All of us can take part.

This page follows the ideas of the XPLORE study, from the Center for Mission Mobilization: five habits of a world Christian, each with a practical step at MovilizarMe.

  • 1. Pray

    Pray for the nations

    Pray for the nations and for the peoples who still do not know Jesus. Do you have a prayer request? Share it with us: we pray for every request we receive.

    Share my request

  • 2. Send

    Sustain those who go

    Sending is sustaining those who go: with gifts, with care and with steady backing. Your donation is processed securely on Mesa Global's official platform. MovilizarMe never receives or stores your card details.

    Support through Mesa Global

  • 3. Welcome

    The nations are already here

    Among neighbors, coworkers and nearby families: welcoming is also mission. A meal, a friendship, an open door.

    I want to welcome: first step

  • 4. Go

    Go where the church is not yet

    Church planting and a new field sought in prayer. Perhaps your place is in the next field.

    See church planting

  • 5. Mobilize

    Awaken others

    Mobilizing is awakening others: XPLORE in a small group, training at CESI, and the podcast for your church.

    Share the podcast

Take the first step

Small groups with XPLORE

We want to open small groups with the XPLORE study. If you would like to take it or bring it to your church, write to us.
