Ministry

Everything we do points to Jesus

Three fronts, one mission: people finding their way back to God.

CESI and church planter training

CESI trains church planters. In the United States it does so through Instituto CESI USA, with the Omega Course as its central training tool.

The training is practical and accompanied: the planter learns while serving in the field, with mentors who walk with him.

Training enrollment uses a secure Google form. Your details are used only for your training.

Do you want to train as a church planter? Write to us or schedule a meeting.

Church planting

We plant churches in Michigan and support churches that are being born or rising again. Today we closely support Iglesia Yo Soy in Kalamazoo.

Sturgis is part of our testimony: we planted and served there, and that story still shapes what we do today.

We are also praying and searching for a new field. If your city or your church needs support to plant, let us talk.
