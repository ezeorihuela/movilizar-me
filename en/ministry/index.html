Three fronts, one mission: people finding their way back to God.

CESI and church planter training

CESI trains church planters through Instituto CESI USA, with the Omega Course as its central tool. The training is practical and accompanied, with mentors who walk with the planter in the field.

Training enrollment uses a secure Google form. Your details are used only for your training.

Do you want to train as a church planter? Write to us or schedule a meeting.

Church planting

We plant churches in Michigan and support churches being born or rising again; today, Iglesia Yo Soy in Kalamazoo. Sturgis is part of our testimony, and we are seeking a new field in prayer.
