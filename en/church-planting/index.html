Church planting

We plant churches that multiply

A new church is not an event: it is a family putting down roots. We plant with local leadership from day one, so the work continues without us.

Our approach

Three marks of every plant

  • 01

    Disciples first

    People transformed by the gospel before programs and numbers.

  • 02

    Local leadership

    The church belongs to its people: we train local leaders from the start.

  • 03

    A healthy handoff

    The planter's success is handing over well: the church stays in local hands and continues.

Our journey

What God has done, and what is next

  • 2016 to 2022

    Sendero, Mexico City

    We pastored in a poor neighborhood on the outskirts and planted two churches. One of them is a church made up only of children, in the Nueva Creación neighborhood.

  • Sturgis

    Iglesia Misionera in Sturgis

    We planted this church in Michigan and handed it to the North Central Region. Today its local pastor, Edgar, leads it, and the congregation continues.

  • Today

    Kalamazoo and the next field

    We support Iglesia Yo Soy in its revitalization and seek the next planting field in prayer.

I planted the seed, Apollos watered it, but God has been making it grow.
