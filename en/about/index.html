About

What MovilizarMe is

MovilizarMe was born from the heart of God, for the dreamers. Its purpose is to awaken a generation that sparks a movement of Latino missionary impact.

We dream global and we impact local: Dream Global, Impact Local. We believe change begins at home, and that every believer has a place in the mission of God.

We are the Gonzalez family serving together. Ezequiel Gonzalez leads this ministry with his family, because without them he could not do it. We serve from Michigan, with our eyes on the nations.

Helping people find their way back to God.

Ayudando a las personas a encontrar su camino de regreso a Dios.

What we do

  • We train church planters with CESI.
  • We plant and support churches.
  • We mobilize the church toward missions.
  • We serve other churches with practical tools, such as live interpretation.
