About

What MovilizarMe is

MovilizarMe was born from the heart of God, for the dreamers: to awaken a generation that sparks a movement of Latino missionary impact.

We are the Gonzalez family serving together, from Michigan with our eyes on the nations. We dream global and impact local.

Helping people find their way back to God.

Ayudando a las personas a encontrar su camino de regreso a Dios.

What we do

  • We train church planters with CESI.
  • We plant and support churches.
  • We mobilize the church toward missions.
  • We serve other churches with practical tools, such as live interpretation.
