Library

A library that works like a library

The MovilizarMe Library gathers Ezequiel's Christian books in his movilizar.me Drive: one single library, organized and cataloged, also made to share with family.

  • Each book is copied and filed in folders by author.
  • The catalog lets you search by author, title, and subject.
  • The library includes a reader, to open the books and pick up where you left off.
  • The content of each file is reviewed, not only its name: no valuable book is lost.

If you are looking for a specific title, write to us and we will check the catalog.

Helping people find their way back to God.
