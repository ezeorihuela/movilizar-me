CESI USA Institute

We train church planters

CESI trains those who will plant. Our Michigan Campus equips planters with the Omega Course, with mentors beside them and roots in their local church.

A proven tool

The Omega Course was born to train planters in Russia after the fall of the Berlin Wall, and from there it reached the Americas. With no denominational affiliation, it has trained hundreds of planters in dozens of countries and several continents.

CESI was started by Ezequiel's father in Mexico City. Today the Michigan Campus continues that same school, now in English and Spanish.

How it works

Training with your feet in your church

  • 1

    Study by modules

    An ordered journey through the Bible, the planter's life and the practice of planting, at your pace and in your language.

  • 2

    Walk with a mentor

    You do not study alone: a mentor reviews your progress, prays with you, and helps you apply each module.

  • 3

    Plant and multiply

    The goal is not a diploma: it is a planted church and a planter able to train others.
