Live interpretation: how it works

Ezequiel programmed this service himself, and we used it in Sturgis. Today we offer it as a MovilizarMe tool for bilingual churches and events.

  1. A room is created for your event or service.
  2. The room screen is projected: one large QR code and the room code, visible to everyone.
  3. Each person scans the QR code with their phone, or joins with the room code, and chooses their language: Spanish or English.
  4. They listen on their phone, with their earphones, while the interpreter translates live.

No one needs special equipment or complicated apps: just their phone. It is ideal for Sunday services, conferences, and events in two languages.
