Tools at the service of the church

MovilizarMe does not only talk about the mission: it also places practical tools in churches' hands.

Live interpretation

Our own simultaneous interpretation service for churches and conferences. Ezequiel programmed it and we used it in Sturgis. The interpreter streams from wherever they are, the screen projects a QR code and a room code, and each person listens on their phone, in their language.

  • 1

    A room is created

    Your church receives a key and creates the room for its service or event.

  • 2

    The card is projected

    A large QR code and the room code, visible to everyone. The card also downloads as an image.

  • 3

    Everyone listens

    They scan the QR or enter with the code, choose Spanish or English, and listen with their earbuds.

No special equipment or complicated apps: just a phone. Ideal for Sunday services, conferences and bilingual events.
